# ADR NNNN: Title

## Status

Proposed | Accepted | Superseded | Rejected

## Context

Name the workload, team size, deployment target, data ownership, operational
constraints, and observed failure or measurement that makes the decision worth
discussing.

Reference concrete repo artifacts such as code paths, docs, migrations,
scripts, runbooks, metrics, or exercises.

## Decision

State the decision in one or two paragraphs. Include what is explicitly out of
scope.

## Alternatives Considered

- Alternative: describe what it would change, why it was not chosen now, and
  what evidence could make it reasonable later.

## Consequences

List the benefits, tradeoffs, and new constraints accepted by this decision.
Avoid claiming the choice is universally best.

## Failure Modes

Name the broken states this decision can create or expose, and how an operator
would recognize them.

## Operational Cost

Describe the process, deployment, monitoring, debugging, migration, local
development, and team coordination cost.

## Rollback Or Reversal

Explain how to undo, disable, fold back, or migrate away from the decision
without pretending rollback is always clean.

## Interview Defense

Write the short defense a learner should be able to give under questioning:
why this choice fits this system, what it costs, and what evidence would change
the decision.
